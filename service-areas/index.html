Delivery planning

Schedules based on location and frequency

Delivery schedules are planned around the customer's location and requested delivery frequency. Scheduled, by-request, and emergency delivery options are available across our service area.

No distance-based changes: delivery fees and minimum orders do not vary based on distance within the service area.
