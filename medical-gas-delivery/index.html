Medical & Dental Gas Delivery in Upstate South Carolina

Ranger Gas Co. delivers certified medical-grade gas cylinders to dental offices, oral surgeons, clinics, surgical centers, care facilities, hospitals, and other healthcare customers throughout the Upstate.

Medical gas delivery at a glance

We deliver certified medical-grade oxygen, nitrogen, breathing air, CO2, liquid oxygen, nitrous oxide, and liquid nitrogen in cylinders. Customers may rent containers from Ranger Gas Co. or request refills for customer-owned cylinders.

Available gases

Certified medical-grade gases

Our medical and dental delivery service includes:

  • Oxygen and liquid oxygen
  • Nitrous oxide
  • Nitrogen and liquid nitrogen
  • Breathing air
  • Carbon dioxide (CO2)

Cylinder and equipment options

Customers may rent gas containers from Ranger Gas Co. or arrange to have certified customer-owned cylinders refilled. We also offer Belmed equipment for medical and dental gas applications.

Tell us what your facility currently uses when requesting an estimate, and our team will help identify an appropriate supply arrangement. Learn about Belmed equipment and support.
