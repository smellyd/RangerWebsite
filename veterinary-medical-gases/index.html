Veterinary Medical Gas Delivery in Upstate South Carolina

Ranger Gas Co. delivers certified medical-grade gas cylinders and offers equipment for veterinary clinics, animal hospitals, specialty practices, shelters, zoos, mobile veterinarians, and other animal care providers.

Veterinary gas delivery at a glance

We deliver certified medical-grade oxygen, nitrogen, breathing air, CO2, liquid oxygen, nitrous oxide, and liquid nitrogen in cylinders. Veterinary customers may rent containers or request refills for customer-owned cylinders.

Available gases

Certified medical-grade gases

  • Oxygen and liquid oxygen
  • Nitrous oxide
  • Nitrogen and liquid nitrogen
  • Breathing air

Cylinder and equipment options

Rent containers or refill cylinders you own

Veterinary customers may rent gas containers from Ranger Gas Co. or arrange to have certified customer-owned cylinders refilled. Equipment is also available for veterinary gas applications.

Tell us about the gases, cylinders, and equipment your facility uses when requesting an estimate so our team can prepare an appropriate supply arrangement. Learn about Belmed equipment and support.

Helium is also available. Ask about helium cylinder supply for your organization's application.
