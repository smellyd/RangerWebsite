Equipment & Replacement Parts

We can provide equipment and replacement parts available in the Belmed catalog. Ask about the exact product or component so our team can confirm availability and prepare an estimate.

Eligible Installation

We install selected components that attach directly to a cylinder or are otherwise separate from plumbing. Installation is not available for products that are part of a plumbed gas system.

Warranty Coordination

Ranger Gas Co. provides warranty support in coordination with Belmed. Contact us with the product information and issue so we can help determine the next step.

Customers We Support

Belmed products are available through Ranger Gas Co. for medical, dental, veterinary, restaurant, hospitality, and other customers whose application fits the equipment.
