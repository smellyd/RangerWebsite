Gas Delivery & Equipment FAQ

Which certified medical-grade gases do you deliver?

We deliver certified medical-grade oxygen, liquid oxygen, nitrous oxide, nitrogen, liquid nitrogen, carbon dioxide, and breathing air for medical, dental, and veterinary customers. Industrial-grade gases are also available.

Do you serve the entire Upstate South Carolina region?

Yes. We serve the entire Upstate region, including Greenville, Spartanburg, Laurens, Anderson, Easley, Pickens, Seneca, and surrounding communities.

Can you supply restaurant CO2 and beer blends?

Yes. We provide CO2 and beer blend delivery for beer and soda systems, with weekly cylinder rentals available for events.

Are estimates free, and are long-term contracts required?

Estimates are free, and customers are not required to sign a long-term contract.

Can you refill customer-owned cylinders?

Yes. We can refill customer-owned cylinders that are certified. Customers may also rent containers from Ranger Gas Co.

How do I request emergency gas delivery?

Call (864) 528-7979. After-hours emergency delivery depends on whether the requested gas is available in our warehouse.

Do you supply helium?

Yes. Helium cylinder supply is available across the industries we serve. Ask about availability for your organization's application.

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?

We typically respond within 1–2 business hours during normal business hours, Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

Which Belmed equipment can you install?

We install eligible components that attach directly to a cylinder or do not require plumbing. We do not install equipment that is part of a plumbed gas system.
